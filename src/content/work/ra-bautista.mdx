# R.A. Bautista Portfolio

**Digital presence strategy for a Senior Graphic Designer & Brand Growth Strategist.**

This project was built to look credible online without requiring recurring hosting fees, using Astro for high performance and strict content architecture.

## Strategy
Unlike traditional corporate portfolios, we adopted a darker, more editorial theme with striking yellow accents to reflect her personal brand, utilizing Geist and Inter typography.

## Outcomes
- **High Performance Score**
- **Bespoke Theme Implementation**
- **Content-Driven Layout**
