# Graceland Farm

**Competitive intelligence and farm positioning strategy.**

A comprehensive report outlining the market positioning and infrastructural roadmap for an agritourism family farm in Indang, Cavite.

## Strategy
Conducted deep comparable farm analysis to establish a baseline for investment and growth. Delivered as a seven-tab Excel workbook.

## Outcomes
- **Market Positioning Strategy**
- **Infrastructure Investment Roadmap**
- **Competitive Intel Report**
